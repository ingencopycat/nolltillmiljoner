# Reference study — before composition

Inspected 18 September 2026. Reference images are research evidence only; they are not used as prototype assets. No fonts or packages installed.

- [Linear, March 2026 product refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh): inspected the actual old/new sidebar image saved in `references/linear-2026-sidebar.png`. Inactive destinations recede; small icons and consistent vertical rhythm let the selected work dominate. Direction B uses quieter navigation and a separate location bar. It does not copy Linear's issue model.
- [Quartr slide comparison](https://quartr.com/features/slide-history-comparison): inspected the document workflow in `references/quartr-comparison.png` and the mobile calendar in `references/quartr-mobile.png`. Company/event scope stays near the document; history remains contextual. Mobile prioritizes one repeated object and limits navigation. Apply contextual evidence beside claims and one mobile reading task, not a thumbnail-heavy document viewer.
- [Vercel typography](https://vercel.com/geist/typography) and [tables](https://vercel.com/geist/table): inspected rendered documentation examples. Distinct display, label and data roles support scanning without adding surfaces. Apply aligned tabular numbers, quiet units and readable row spacing; avoid presenting financial data as disconnected cards.
- [FT editorial typography proposal](https://github.com/Financial-Times/o-typography/issues/203): a historical primary reference, **not** claimed as a current 2026 product inspection. Separating reading typography from UI typography is useful for Direction A. Current Origami pages were unavailable. No FT font, branding or layout is copied.

## Composition hypotheses, before tokens

A: a broad report masthead; serif company and reading typography; a financial abstract above an integrated history figure; a narrow editorial margin for source/period. Public analysis should look like a published research essay.

B: a quiet left work rail; compact location and company headers; the selected financial measure owns the chart, with other measures in aligned rows; thesis is a writing canvas with a contextual review inspector. Public reading hides most workspace machinery.

C: a recognisable split of company identity and a large financial numeral; broad green/ink tonal regions and numbered research sections; a compact horizontal spine of navigation. Public analysis pairs an emphatic company masthead with a restrained authored essay. On mobile the numeral, then evidence, becomes the sequence.

Each hypothesis must be rendered, criticised and revised before provisional tokens are documented. These are three alternatives, not a proposed combined system.
