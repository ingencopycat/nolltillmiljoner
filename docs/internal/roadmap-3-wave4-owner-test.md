# Wave 4 owner review

Engineering fixtures and browser automation are not external-user validation. Record reviewer, date, build revision, device, observations and accepted/needs-change for each task. Do not record private portfolio data. Authority: Master Roadmap 3.0 §5.4, §5.9, §5.12, §7 and §14.

1. Open `research.html?ticker=NVDA`. Ask the reviewer to explain the difference between revenue growth and operating-margin movement. Open **Visa underlag och metod**, reconstruct one percentage and one percentage-point difference, then follow the reported source. Confirm that the page does not answer whether the stock is attractive. Review statement method `ntm-fundamental/1`, especially the annual-duration tolerance and R6 display tolerance.
2. Repeat with SOFI, CRWD, CRWV and FLY. Ask why SOFI has no industrial FCF/margin interpretation, why a negative operating margin is still a reported ratio, and why one annual observation cannot establish growth. Check each missing component against its existing stock file and source; do not fill a missing field to improve the presentation. Confirm that an unavailable state is understood as an evidence limitation, not a neutral company assessment.
3. Expand **Per aktie**. Every existing pilot has an unverified annual share basis. Ask the reviewer to identify what evidence is missing before comparing total-profit and EPS growth. The synthetic eligible R6 fixture is an engineering example, not evidence that any current company passes. An independent financial-method reviewer should validate numerator attribution, diluted denominator and split history before approving future real R6 eligibility.
4. Save a local test thesis, inspect its original snapshot and use the automated correction fixture in `scripts/wave4_browser.py`. Confirm that an annual accession correction shows old/new evidence, can be acknowledged through the existing review decision, stays quiet for identical evidence and leaves the first revision untouched. Fetch timestamp changes must not be called corrections. R12 must not appear in Min NTM merely because private prose mentions a metric.
5. Open Macro on desktop and at 390px, in both themes. Select a week, expand an event and explain actual/forecast/previous, source time versus Stockholm date, and unknown revision history. Open Knowledge and return with Escape: focus, selected week and position should remain. Inspect a partial/failed-refresh fixture. Ask the reviewer to identify the retained value's date instead of calling the attempted refresh fresh data.
6. Open Earnings. Ask what the calendar covers, whether the issuer confirmed the date, what before/after means, and when the schedule was last verified. Correct answers: the image's selected companies; issuer confirmation and last issuer check unknown; US market session in America/New_York. The transcription is not comprehensive coverage or a live verified schedule. Inspect an archived week and a missing-current-week state.
7. Editorially review the single new Knowledge object `macro-releases` in the canonical catalog. Check its CPI/PCE/payroll/BNP intent, primary-source claim map, synthetic arithmetic, units, limitations, duplicate comparison, reviewer/date/version and pending acceptance. Read the existing inflation/rates/FCF/EPS explanations used by the flows; no duplicate definitions were added to the calendar code.
8. Confirm who monitors the existing correction contact in `om-metod.html#ansvar` (Instagram ingencopycat), who triages financial/calendar errors, the response/check cadence and backup owner. This implementation neither verifies monitoring nor creates a mailbox. Substantive errors should identify statement version, company/event, period, old/new source and affected calculation, following `om-metod.html#rattelser`.

For a small formative pilot, ask consenting participants to perform tasks 1–3 and 5–6 without prompting. Record literal explanations and navigation failures rather than a satisfaction score. No pilot recruitment, owner approval, independent financial review or user comprehension evidence has occurred in this implementation. Engineering release readiness is separate from these decisions.
