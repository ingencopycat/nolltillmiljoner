# Site-wide canonical Visual V3 rollout

The owner-approved production Visual V3 is now applied to all 121 styled production routes. One additional route is a bare calculator redirect. No new visual direction or product system was introduced. Nothing committed, pushed or deployed.

1. **Migration inventory.** [Migration map](visual-v3-sitewide-inventory.md) and [machine-readable inventory](../qa/visual-v3/sitewide/inventory.json) were created before implementation. Families: 59 Academy, 27 Knowledge, 30 general pages, three social pages, two approved Research surfaces and one unstyled redirect. Shared CSS and generators were identified before rollout; 80 before-state captures were retained.
2. **Global architecture.** All styled routes load `visual-v3-tokens.css` and use `body.visual-v3`. Canonical tokens have one palette owner; superseded root palettes were removed. `premium.css` remains the shared component/composition owner, with existing family stylesheets. No framework or visual-effect runtime was added. The approved `research-v3.css` remains byte-identical to the rollout baseline.
3. **Shell/header/footer.** Shared Midnight/Electric canvas, original logo, serif wordmark, 91/71px desktop/mobile header, circular theme control, accent selected navigation and quiet ruled footer. Existing account identity, menu, keyboard behavior, links and information architecture remain.
4. **Homepage.** Editorial serif introduction, clear primary calculator action, secondary Research action and quiet continuation link. Removed enclosing hero/today cards and nested market-status fills. Real widgets, macro event priority, exact hidden-event counts, expanded events and earnings coverage remain unchanged and tested.
5. **Calculator system.** Shared open form sections, canonical controls, financial serif result values, ruled result groups, restrained secondary results, tabs, tables and existing progressive depth. Required inputs and interpretation-changing assumptions remain visible. No formulas changed. Actual calculated compound/FIRE results are captured, not just untouched forms.
6. **Calculator exceptions.** All 12 production calculator routes were audited: compound/dividend, FIRE, savings goal, fees, leverage, recovery, mortgage, ISK, stock valuation, return, average purchase cost and currency-adjusted return. Compound/dividend retain modes and year-by-year returns; FIRE retains stress views; savings goal retains follow-up; mortgage/ISK retain their rule context; comparison/dense tables retain local scrolling. Research valuation remains in its approved workspace.
7. **Min NTM.** Small documented UX adaptation: review queue, active theses, saved plans and recent tools now precede learning progress and optional decision-method infrastructure. Account entry is a quiet link. DOM order matches reading/keyboard order; section IDs, storage, review semantics and backup are unchanged. Empty and returning/due workspaces are captured.
8. **Academy.** Shared serif hierarchy and open roadmap/entity sections replace rounded card accumulation. Current/next action is visually promoted above XP metadata. Learning paths, skills, levels, progress and completion behavior remain unchanged.
9. **Lessons/activities.** Approximately 800px reading width, serif lead/body, visible examples, ruled exercises and source depth. Interactive radio/input forms remain intact. Metadata and quiz controls remain sans-serif. Activities are not converted to article-only pages; progress and next-step controls still work.
10. **Knowledge.** Existing search, filters and collapsed categories remain. Canonical search controls, editorial titles and open immediate-answer sections replace box-heavy presentation. Answer content, source links and Connected Experience destinations are unchanged.
11. **Fråga NTM.** Deterministic reviewed retrieval is unchanged; no model calls or AI framing. Captures include a submitted question and the scrolled result, alongside the minimal initial state. Existing abstention and reviewed-source behavior passes its focused and full tests.
12. **Account.** Healthy identity/sync sections remain quiet; error, conflict, privacy and destructive flows retain clear status/controls. Canonical fields, dialogs and ruled sections replace rounded dashboard shells. No account permission or sync changes.
13. **Public Profile.** Serif display name and published-company headings frame existing analyses; bio, username, role, dates and follower controls remain subordinate. Existing initials marker remains; no uploads, reputation, portfolios or new social fields. Security captures intentionally show escaped hostile text as text.
14. **Upptäck Research.** Existing company/report rows receive editorial headings and compact metadata. Empty/sparse local state is honest. Populated public/profile/discovery contract captures come from the existing intercepted test service, not fabricated production activity.
15. **Macro.** Open calendar/day hierarchy and quieter metrics/sidebar surfaces; date/time, event, importance, actual/forecast/previous and source statuses remain. Existing partial-source warning and unavailable future schedules remain explicit. No source logic changed.
16. **Earnings.** Canonical page/reading hierarchy around the existing licensed/reference image and readable transcription. Coverage and verification limitations remain. Capture runner waits for visible images to decode so the gallery shows actual content rather than a transient blank image box.
17. **Trust/methodology.** Reading width, serif prose, strong headings, quiet separators and source links. Required privacy, correction/version and calculation disclosures remain. No legal or financial content rewrite.
18. **Editorial/resources.** Article shells are open; long-form reading uses editorial typography. Independent resource entities retain compact 3px surfaces and existing creator content. Original media retains its content/branding; no decorative finance imagery added.
19. **Shared components.** Buttons, fields, search, tabs, disclosures, dialogs, status, tables, result values, links and pagination use canonical roles. Card geometry is restrained. Existing loading/error/empty state messages and announcements remain. The disabled, destructive and semantic status roles remain distinct.
20. **Typography.** Georgia/Times New Roman for display, editorial reading and financial figures; Segoe UI/Arial for controls, metadata and ordinary interface text. Size follows purpose: display homepage, smaller task headings, readable lesson/answer lead, 57px-cap financial results. System fonts only.
21. **Color/theme.** Frozen A2.1 light/dark palettes. Brand text and actions no longer inherit legacy green. Positive/success semantics remain green. Calculated-state banners are informational rather than success-green. Historical edge colors provide distinct secondary chart series; `script.js` changes only this presentation lookup.
22. **Card/container reduction.** Removed enclosing homepage/today wrappers, general page-card surfaces, calculator form cards, personal workspace cards, article cards, Knowledge immediate-answer boxes and redundant learning panel fills. Kept independent selectable resources, examples, distinct status contexts and dialogs. Semantic HTML grouping remains.
23. **Mobile.** Representative surfaces captured at 360/390/430px in both themes; every styled route also audited at 390px in both themes. No whole-page horizontal overflow. Existing 320px and zoom checks pass. First task/identity remains above optional infrastructure; dense tables retain local scrolling.
24. **Light mode.** Intentional pale canvas, petrol actions, legible secondary text and thin rules. Specific light-theme card selectors were migrated as well as dark defaults; this avoided old boxed wrappers winning through specificity.
25. **Dark mode.** Midnight canvas, restrained cyan actions, historical gray-blue chart series, serif hierarchy and quiet surfaces. Removed legacy gradients and green action glow; no animation library or neon treatment.
26. **Accessibility.** Full browser suite preserves keyboard, skip links, labels, focus return, disclosures, dialogs, touch targets and reduced motion. Practical accessibility/CSP checks pass on 36 page/views. All 242 route/theme checks pass canvas activation, reflow and visible primary text contrast; sampled primary minimum 7.12:1. Chart alternatives and table semantics remain. Automated checks do not certify screen-reader/physical-device behavior.
27. **Performance.** Total CSS source change across existing files: approximately +2.69KB, after legacy removal. Shared chart lookup adds 54 normalized source bytes; all other production runtime JS is unchanged. Most formerly V2 pages add one small local canonical CSS request; Research already loaded it. No new fonts, images, framework, third-party calls or chart dependency. Cold uncompressed localhost measurements and per-asset inventories are in `quality.json`; representative earlier final sample DOMContentLoaded was approximately 140–246ms for Knowledge/Academy/compound/home. No field speed or Core Web Vitals claim. Existing shared script/vendor/data payloads still dominate.
28. **Old CSS removed.** Deleted superseded root/light palette blocks from `style.css` and `premium.css`; replaced old gradients, large fixed radii and green action shadows. Updated existing family components rather than adding a second site-wide override stylesheet. Retained legacy geometry still used by production and independent semantic success styling.
29. **Visual QA/self-critique.** Batch renders caught light-theme wrapper specificity, equal-weight learning progress, boxed calendar pages, weak primary result hierarchy and residual green calculated banners. Final review moved Min NTM work ahead of learning/infrastructure and separated chart series colors. Before gallery: 80 states. Final primary gallery: 160 states plus Fråga/thesis captures. Additional real calculated/returning states, 31 social/account regression images and current canonical Research/thesis/public captures. [QA hub](../qa/visual-v3/sitewide/index.html).
30. **Files changed.** Machine-readable task-baseline changes are in `changed-files.json` (126 production files: predominantly HTML stylesheet/body activation and generated pages). Presentation owners: `style.css`, `premium.css`, `visual-v3-tokens.css`, `academy.css`, `knowledge.css`, `social.css`, one chart-color line in `script.js`, Min NTM section order, Academy/Knowledge generators. Also test expectations, QA scripts, specification, inventory/report and artifacts. Earlier uncommitted Research/design work remains preserved.
31. **Tests changed.** Browser and social theme assertions now expect the frozen canonical colors on every migrated page; contrast, interaction and state assertions remain. Video static-render comparison normalizes CRLF/LF only, retaining the complete-content assertion; regeneration exposed this existing cross-platform mismatch. Added all-route/style/contrast, multi-surface capture and real-work-state QA scripts. No functional assertion was removed to make a visual change pass.

Full browser: 38 pass; final changed workspace flows and chart palette/control tests also pass. Social and SDK/session suites pass; session run initially timed out and passed unchanged on retry. Public/account populated captures use existing test intercepts; a bare local server still shows honest cloud-disabled states. Physical mobile keyboards, Safari/Firefox, assistive technology and field performance remain unverified. Third-party widgets and retained earnings artwork own their internal presentation.
