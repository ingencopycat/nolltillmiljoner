## Maintaining B36

One source/dataset per record. Split a source when its datasets have different
rights. `repositoryEvidence` establishes observed use, never legal permission.
`termsEvidence` is a review pointer; it does not mean every permission has been
verified. `reviewDate` records this inventory review; `nextReview` is an internal
reminder, not a legal expiration date. Unclear fields say **Needs verification**.
Unknown rights block expansion, monetization and new redistribution of that data.
This register records existing risk; it does not retroactively clear existing use.

Founder first review: private macro publishers, Earnings Whispers images and
transcriptions, FRED CSV/series conditions, TradingView widget attribution, then
SEC filing exhibits and third-party video material. Ask for the exact rights for
public anonymous display, cached static JSON, backup/export, transformations and
any future AI processing. Archive dated terms and written answers privately; record
the evidence reference, scope, reviewer and next review here without secrets.
Review before every dataset addition, vendor change or paid feature and at least
quarterly. Price quotes and usage limits expire; record currency, tax basis,
minimum term, exchange surcharges, overages and quote validity.

BLS statistical-publication scope is based on its
[copyright statement](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm).
SEC's rate entry is based on its
[access policy](https://www.sec.gov/about/privacy-information).
FRED needs a distinct service and originating-series review under its
[terms](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/terms/).
These are diligence notes, not legal advice.

## Free-first decision rule

“If it costs money every month before we have users or revenue, pause unless it is essential.”

No recurring spend is authorized by this pack. Every dependency must have a named
feature, free/manual alternative, observed benefit, purchase trigger, monthly
ceiling and removal plan. Start all ceilings at **0 SEK/month**. Owner may set a
nonzero ceiling only after evidence and rights review, using the economics model.
Suggested experiment guardrail, not a fact: total new recurring dependencies at
most 10% of trailing three-month subscription revenue ex VAT, and never more than
the expected incremental contribution. With no revenue, pause or document why
an essential dependency cannot be replaced manually. No minimum annual contract
merely to obtain an advertised monthly rate.
