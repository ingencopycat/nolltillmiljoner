# B56 — NTM Pro pilot hypothesis

Value hypothesis: make it easier to return to your own reasoning and understand
what changed. No promise of better investment returns. Subscription is one test,
not an assumed business model. Do not advertise unbuilt features as available.

| Free commitment | Possible future Pro experiment |
| --- | --- |
| Current local theses and tools, current local review queue, basic reminders and supported Research | Cross-device sync after accounts exist; advanced queue/filtering; longer organized review history |
| Correctness, source transparency, export, deletion and basic privacy controls | Additional company coverage only after licensing/data quality review |
| Existing saved local data remains accessible; no newly imposed thesis cap | More organized active theses only if a limit is validated; do not retroactively lock existing records |
| Existing Change Detection remains useful | Advanced “what changed?”/report-relevance workflow tested first as a manual evidence comparison |

AI “what changed?” is a future conditional experiment only, after a deterministic
comparison proves insufficient, data/AI rights are verified, quality can be
evaluated and a per-user cost cap is funded. No generic AI, provider or integration
is added. Basic reminders are already local; paid delivery must add proven value,
not degrade them. Cloud sync requires a separate security, migration and privacy
project. The pilot is currently a founder-operated study using existing features.

## Concrete experiment

Recruit 10–15 consenting activated participants from B52. First run free for two
genuine review opportunities. Founder may manually help find public sources and
observe friction, with help recorded. Offer only a written prototype description
of the smallest desired package; label every unbuilt capability. No checkout,
card details, invoice, auto-renewal, fake purchase button or paid waitlist is used.

Prices **79 / 129 / 199 SEK per month including assumed VAT** are test hypotheses,
not a public offer. Start one small cohort at 129, then test 79 or 199 with a
subsequent comparable cohort after recording the package unchanged. Avoid claiming
statistical price elasticity from tiny samples. Ask which existing tool expense
or recurring manual task this package would replace, why they last paid/cancelled,
and invite a specific later opt-in conversation. Record actual past behavior and
follow-through separately from verbal enthusiasm. A no-payment study cannot prove
paid conversion; label all “willingness to pay” evidence accordingly.

Suggested precommitted gates: 6/10 activate unaided; 4/6 eligible complete two
independent reviews; at least 3 retained participants show an existing time/money
tradeoff attributable to the proposed package. Review after two opportunities,
report denominators and prompted visits, and extend when insufficient eligible
participants exist. Pass means permission to design a later paid test, not launch.
Fail if only reminders from the founder cause returns, private data cannot be
protected, quality depends on a paywall, or conservative contribution is negative.
Iterate one workflow once before considering a broader package.

Before payment: demonstrate implemented package reliability, rights and recurring
cost ceiling; assign support capacity; test export/deletion/cancellation; establish
accurate billing/VAT/refund terms with qualified review; obtain explicit owner
launch authorization. A later paid test should evaluate actual opt-in conversion,
contribution after support and retained use through two billing periods, with a
predeclared cohort, price and stop date. None is measured by this pack.

Cancellation principles: clear monthly price and renewal timing, easy cancellation
without contacting the founder, no surprise annual commitment, published fair
refund policy, no deletion as punishment for cancelling. Explain the paid-feature
end date; preserve export and a reasonable disclosed retrieval period for future
cloud data. Collect optional cancellation reasons without blocking the action.
Do not promise indefinite free cloud storage; local exports remain usable.

No sponsor outreach, affiliate contract or ad integration occurs here. Treat their
revenue as uncertain, separate inputs in B57; do not spend fixed costs against an
uncontracted sponsor. Subscription, sponsorship, affiliate and ads can each fail.
