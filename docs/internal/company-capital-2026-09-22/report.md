**Model and extraction.** Generic metric identities retain unit/currency, instant/quarter/YTD periods, scope, definition version, share basis, revision state and explicit calculation eligibility. 184 exact inline-XBRL facts preserve concept, entity, dimensions, period, unit and canonical hash; two untagged bank ratios and three funding passages use pinned text. Unsupported transforms, conflicting duplicates, changed passages or missing facts fail closed. Production refresh revalidated official SEC documents; source failure retains the previous complete feed.

**History and comparability.** Four 10-Qs per issuer cover June/July 2025–June/July 2026, with five balance dates and four selected quarterly expense/share periods. Q4 expense flows are not inferred. CrowdStrike's original and issuer-restated 4-for-1 share observations coexist; only three contiguous split-compatible balance dates are charted, with a valid issuer-restated YoY comparison. Its prior-year SBC correction is also preserved: $287.153m originally versus $279.631m revised. Missing periods, changed definitions and incompatible YTD lengths break comparisons.

**Derived measures.** Only comparable share-count YoY, same-quarter expensed SBC / GAAP revenue (SoFi: net revenue), and industrial cash minus total carrying borrowings. The latter explicitly excludes securities, restricted cash, leases and operating liabilities; it is not presented as unrestricted net liquidity. Every calculation exposes inputs and method. No dilution inference from SBC or weighted-share differences.

**Presentation and Research.** Visual V3 uses open numeric rows, zero-based share/cash/debt bars and progressive issuance/funding detail. Exact history, original/revised values, definitions and SEC sources sit under **Källor & metod**. Existing `asOf`, `compare` and `changesSince` APIs expose capital evidence, corrections and scope breaks without modifying saved Research revisions. No final “Sedan din analys” interface.
